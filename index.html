BEFORE YOU GO ✳

A few things to know.

01 / GETTING THERE

Book the morning flight

HCMC → Da Nang on Nov 9 isn’t in the original notes. A morning flight gives you the most time in Da Nang.

02 / THE BIG TRANSFER

Make room for rest

The overnight bus from Ninh Binh to Ha Giang before the Nov 15 loop start is the toughest stretch. Confirm the timing and bring a sleep kit.

03 / HEADING HOME

Protect your buffer day

The Nov 17 journey back to HCMC is long, with a flight via Hanoi. Confirm connections; keep Nov 18 free before your Nov 19 morning flight.
